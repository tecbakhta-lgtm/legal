## 4. Data that stays on your device

The app is local-first. The following is created and stored exclusively on your device, in a local SQLite database (`dhikr.db`) within the app's private storage, and is **not** transmitted to us or to anyone else:

- The display name you optionally enter, used only to greet you in the app.
- Your adhkar and their content (title, Arabic text, transliteration, translation, source, repetition count, time of day, icon, colour), including your edits to the adhkar shipped with the app.
- Daily completions and their timestamps, streaks, statistics and usage duration recorded locally.
- Tasbih items and counters.
- Your categories, ordering, reminder times, language, theme, numeral system, week start, reader appearance, sound and vibration preferences, and your crash-reporting choice.

A random installation identifier (a UUID) is generated on your device and stored in the operating system's secure store (Keychain on iOS, the encrypted preferences store on Android). It is described in section 5.

We have no server for this data, no copy of it, and no technical means of accessing it.

## 5. Data we receive: crash diagnostics

Crash reporting is **off by default**. On first launch the app asks you to make an explicit choice, and nothing is transmitted unless and until you enable it. When crash reporting is enabled, and only then, the following is transmitted to our processor Sentry (section 8) when the app crashes or encounters an unhandled error:

| Data | What it contains | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Error diagnostics | The error type and message, the stack trace, and — for interface errors — the React component stack | Identify and fix the defect |
| Application identifiers | App version and build, release and distribution identifiers, and the environment tag (`production`) | Attribute the report to the correct release |
| Device and system context | Technical context collected automatically by the Sentry SDK about the device and the app at the moment of failure, such as device model, operating-system version, app state, locale, time zone and memory or storage state | Reproduce and diagnose the defect |
| Session records | Basic session records (session start and whether it ended in a crash), used to compute crash-free rates | Measure stability |
| Breadcrumbs | A short technical trail of preceding events, including internal diagnostic messages logged by the app | Understand the sequence leading to the crash |
| Anonymous installation identifier | The random identifier described in section 4, attached as the report's user reference. It contains no name, email address, telephone number or account, and we hold nothing that links it to you | Group reports originating from the same installation |

**Performance and behavioural monitoring are disabled** (trace sampling is set to zero), so no screen views, taps, navigation analytics, timings or user-behaviour profiles are collected.

We do not include the content of your adhkar, your name or your records in crash reports, and the app is not designed to place them there. Because diagnostic messages are generated by the software rather than selected by us, we cannot guarantee that a technical error message never contains a fragment of data that caused the error. Any such fragment is processed only for the diagnostic purposes above.

Your device's IP address is necessarily visible to the receiving servers as an unavoidable feature of any internet transmission. The app is not configured to attach the IP address, or any other personally identifying network data, to the report itself.

Reports are transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

## 6. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Processing of crash diagnostics is based on your **consent**, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and UK GDPR, given through the in-app crash-reporting setting. You may withdraw consent at any time (section 7); withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

We carry out no other processing of personal data as controller. Data described in section 4 is processed by you, on your own device, and is not accessible to us.

## 7. Your choice, and how to change it

- The crash-reporting setting is presented on first launch and can be changed at any time in **Settings → Crash reports**.
- Declining, or leaving the setting off, results in **zero transmission**: no crash report, no session record and no identifier leaves the device.
- Switching the setting **off** stops transmission immediately and removes the installation identifier from any future report. Reports already received are not automatically deleted; see section 11 to request erasure.
- Switching the setting **on** applies to new reports and takes effect no later than the next app start.

## 8. Processor

We use **Sentry** (operated by Functional Software, Inc., trading as Sentry) exclusively as a **processor** (service provider) to receive, store and display crash diagnostics on our behalf and on our documented instructions. Sentry does not use this data for its own purposes, and we do not permit it to sell or share the data or to use it for advertising.

Crash data for this app is processed in Sentry's **United States** region. Sentry's privacy notice: https://sentry.io/privacy/

We use no other processor, no analytics provider, no advertising network, no attribution or measurement service, no data broker, and no other external API. Apart from the crash reports described above, the app makes no network requests of its own and contains no embedded web content.

The application is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those platforms process data about the download, installation and — where applicable — platform-level crash collection under their own privacy notices, as independent controllers, outside our control.

## 9. Notifications

Reminders are **local notifications scheduled on the device** by the operating system. No push tokens are generated or registered, no notification content is sent to us or to any server, and no push messages are received from us.

## 10. Backups you create, and external links

- **Export:** you can create a backup file (JSON) containing all of your app data, including your name if you entered one. The file is written to the app's own storage and then either shared through the system share sheet or saved to a folder you select. The destination is entirely your choice; we neither receive the file nor have visibility of it. Once the file leaves the app, it is protected only by the storage or service you send it to.
- **Import:** you can restore data by selecting a backup file through the system file picker. The file is read on the device only. The app requests no broad access to your storage, media or photo library.
- **External link:** the Sources screen contains a link to `hisnmuslim.com`, which opens in your external browser. Once you follow it, that website's own privacy practices apply.

## 12. Retention

- **Crash diagnostics:** retained by Sentry for the retention period applicable to our account and then deleted automatically. We do not retain separate copies elsewhere.
- **Device data:** persists on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. The installation identifier remains in the operating system's secure store until the app's stored data is removed by the operating system; it is not deleted merely by switching crash reporting off.

## 13. International transfers

If you enable crash reporting from the EEA or the UK, the resulting crash diagnostics are transferred to the United States for processing by Sentry. That transfer takes place on the basis of the data-processing terms and transfer mechanism offered by Sentry to its customers, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, for the United Kingdom, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

## 14. Security

Crash diagnostics are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On the device, app data is held in the application's private storage area protected by the operating system's application sandbox and device-level encryption, and the installation identifier is held in the operating system's secure store. No security measure is absolute, and we cannot guarantee the security of a device that is compromised, rooted or jailbroken, or of a backup file after you have exported it.

## 15. Permissions

The app requests only the following:

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` on Android 13 and above; the notification permission prompt on iOS) — to display the reminders you schedule.
- **Start after device restart** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` on Android) — to restore your scheduled reminders after the device is restarted.
- **Access to a folder or file you select**, at the moment you export or import a backup, granted through the operating system's own file picker and only for the file or folder you choose.

The app requests no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photo library, Bluetooth, NFC, body sensors, call or SMS data, or installed-app lists.
